## What we do

PDFCraftTool is an independent website about **PdfCraft**, the free and open-source PDF editor built by the ArtCraft team. We help you get PdfCraft running and decide whether it can replace a paid PDF subscription. On this site you will find:

- **PdfCraft downloads** for Windows, macOS, Linux, FreeBSD and the web, always linked to the release files on github.com/storytold/pdfcraft;
- **Installation guides** for every platform, including how to verify a download with its SHA-256 checksum;
- **Tutorials** for everyday tasks: reading, searching, organizing pages, combining and splitting files, forms, passwords and the command line;
- **A feature comparison with Adobe Acrobat**, covering price, platforms, privacy and what PdfCraft can't do yet;
- **Hands-on notes** from using PdfCraft on real documents, and a hosted copy of PdfCraft Online so you can try it in your browser.

## Who maintains this site

PDFCraftTool.org is maintained by the independent publisher of this website. We write and maintain the guides, check download information and handle corrections through [support@pdfcrafttool.org](mailto:support@pdfcrafttool.org). We do not develop the PdfCraft application.

## How we work

Our sources are the official PdfCraft repository, release notes and published download assets. Download version information is refreshed from GitHub during site builds. Articles distinguish documented behavior from checks we actually performed; a hands-on result identifies the tested version, method and sample so readers can understand its limits. We do not claim to test every release or every platform. Acrobat prices and features come from adobe.com and are dated on the comparison page. We point out PdfCraft's limits as clearly as its strengths, because an honest comparison is more useful than a sales pitch.

## Independence

PDFCraftTool.org is an independent guide to PdfCraft and is not affiliated with ArtCraft or Adobe. We do not charge for PdfCraft, repackage it or modify it. Every download goes to the release ArtCraft publishes on GitHub. This website is also unrelated to the [PDFCraftTool/pdfcraft project on GitHub](https://github.com/PDFCraftTool/pdfcraft), which is a different project maintained by a different team. For the official project, visit [getartcraft.com/apps/pdfcraft](https://getartcraft.com/apps/pdfcraft) or the [PdfCraft GitHub repository](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft).

## Contact

Found an outdated guide, a broken link or a mistake in the Acrobat comparison? Have a question about installing PdfCraft? Email us at [support@pdfcrafttool.org](mailto:support@pdfcrafttool.org). We usually reply within 2 business days. See our [contact page](/contact) for what to include in a correction report.

Bug reports and feature requests for the PdfCraft software itself are best sent to the official [PdfCraft issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft/issues) or the [ArtCraft Discord](https://discord.gg/artcraft), where the developers can act on them.
