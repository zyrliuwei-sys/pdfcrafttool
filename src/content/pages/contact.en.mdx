## Get in touch

For questions about this website or a correction to one of our guides, email **support@pdfcrafttool.org**.

[Send an email to support@pdfcrafttool.org](mailto:support@pdfcrafttool.org)

We usually reply within 2 business days.

## What to report

- **Content corrections:** an inaccurate explanation, a missing qualification or instructions that do not match the application.
- **Outdated version information:** a newer release, a changed feature or an installation step that needs updating.
- **Broken links:** a download, source reference or internal page that no longer opens.

Please include the page URL, the passage or link concerned, and what you expected to happen. For installation questions, mention your operating system and PdfCraft version. A link to an official release note or documentation page helps us check a correction.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or confidential PDFs. A description of the issue or a small anonymized example is usually enough.

## Website questions and software support

This inbox is maintained by the publisher of PDFCraftTool.org. We can correct our articles and download information. We do not develop PdfCraft and cannot promise fixes to the application itself. Software bug reports and feature requests can be sent to the official [PdfCraft issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft/issues).

PDFCraftTool.org is an independent guide to PdfCraft and is not affiliated with ArtCraft or Adobe. Read more about our sources and maintenance practices on the [About page](/about), or return to the [homepage](/).
